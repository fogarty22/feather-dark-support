Feather Dark

Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Feather Dark is made by Patrick Fogarty. The extension changes website colors in Safari. It does not collect or send data to the developer or to another service.

What happens on your Mac

The extension uses the current website's hostname to find your site preference. Automatic mode checks the page's background to decide whether to apply dark styling. These operations happen on your Mac.

Feather Dark stores your on/off choice and any website settings you select in local extension storage. Site settings include the corresponding hostname. These preferences are not transmitted to the developer.

What the extension does not collect

Feather Dark does not collect or upload browsing history, page contents, account information, cookies, personal information, or usage analytics. The extension has no ads, tracking tools, or third-party analytics services, and makes no network requests.

Website access

Safari asks for website access so Feather Dark can apply its stylesheet to pages you allow. That permission does not send your browsing data anywhere. You can review or revoke website access in Safari Settings > Extensions.

This website and support requests

This support website is separate from the extension. The website host receives normal connection information when you visit, such as your IP address and the pages requested. This site adds no analytics, advertising, forms, or browser storage.

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. If you use GitHub, GitHub receives the information needed to provide its service under its privacy policy. Information you post in a public issue is visible to others. Do not include passwords, account details, or private browsing information.

Patrick Fogarty can read and respond to the support information you choose to post. The extension itself does not send a support report or attach any browsing data.

Your choices

Use the toolbar popup to disable dark mode or change a website's setting. You can also disable or uninstall the extension through Safari's settings. You do not need an account to use Feather Dark.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue in the support repository.
