App Store release in preparation. $0.99 once. No subscription.

A choice for each site

Use Automatic, Always darken, or Leave alone. Feather Dark remembers your choice for the website you're visiting.

Small by design

A stylesheet and a small script do the work. There is no background page, polling timer, or repeated scan of the page.

Your preferences stay local

No accounts, ads, analytics, or network requests from the extension. Only your on/off choice and site settings are stored locally.

Easy to turn off

Some websites need their own colors. Choose Leave alone when a site doesn't look right, or use the main switch to turn dark mode off.
